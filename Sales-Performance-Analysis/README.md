## 📌 Project Overview

Sales Performance Analysis is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate sales performance, product profitability, category-wise sales, and monthly sales trends.

The project focuses on transforming sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data transformation, KPI tracking, data visualization, and interactive dashboard development.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, product costs, and total profit.
- Identify top-performing products by sales and profit.
- Evaluate monthly sales trends and product costs.
- Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
- Analyze geographical sales distribution across states.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business performance monitoring.

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Total Sales:** The dashboard reports total sales of 2.30M.
- **Total Product Cost:** Product costs account for 2.01M in the displayed results.
- **Total Profit:** The reported total profit is 286.40K.
- **Sales by Category:** Technology contributes 836.15K (36.4%), Furniture contributes 742K (32.3%), and Office Supplies contributes 719.05K (31.3%) of sales.
- **Profit by Category:** Office Supplies contributes 145.46K (50.79%), Technology contributes 122.49K (42.77%), and Furniture contributes 18.45K (6.44%) of profit.
- **Monthly Trends:** The dashboard compares monthly sales and product costs to help identify changes in performance throughout the year.

*Insights are based on the values visible in the dashboard. Figures may vary with filters.*
