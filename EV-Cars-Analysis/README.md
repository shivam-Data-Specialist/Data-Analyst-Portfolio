## 📌 Project Overview

Electric Vehicle Analytics is a Power BI data analytics project designed to analyze the electric vehicle (EV) market, vehicle specifications, sales performance, safety ratings, battery capacity, driving range, and charging performance.

The project transforms EV data into interactive visualizations and business insights to help understand market trends, compare manufacturers, and evaluate electric vehicle performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze annual electric vehicle sales and market trends.
- Compare brands based on sales, safety ratings, and vehicle specifications.
- Evaluate battery capacity, driving range, charging speed, and performance.
- Identify top-performing EV brands and models.
- Explore vehicle distribution by body type, segment, and drivetrain.
- Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven decision-making.

## 🏆 Key Insights

- **Safety ratings:** Volvo leads the displayed brand comparison with an average safety rating of 4.75, followed by Rivian at 4.62 and Porsche at 4.57.
- **Battery capacity:** Honda shows an average battery capacity of 93 kWh, followed by Mercedes at 83 kWh and Porsche at 78 kWh.
- **Market trend:** The annual EV count increases from 30 in 2020 to 70 in 2025 in the displayed chart.
- **Vehicle performance:** The dashboard compares charging speed, top speed, torque, horsepower, acceleration, and driving range across EV brands.
- **Pricing analysis:** Brand-wise price comparisons help explore differences in the EV market.

*Insights reflect the values visible in the dashboard and may change with filters.*
