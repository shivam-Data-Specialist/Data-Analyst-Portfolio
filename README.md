# EV Cars Market Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing electric vehicle specifications, brand performance, driving range, battery capacity, charging speed, and safety ratings.

## Project Objectives
- Compare EV brands and models.
- Analyse annual sales and vehicle counts.
- Explore battery capacity and driving range.
- Compare charging speeds and safety ratings.
- Identify patterns in electric vehicle specifications.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for sales, price, charging speed, battery capacity, and range.
- Interactive year slicer.
- Brand, model, drive type, segment, and variant filters.
- Brand-wise comparisons of range, battery capacity, and safety ratings.
- Year-wise EV count analysis.

## Key Insights
1. **Annual Sales:** The dashboard displays total annual sales of 259M.
2. **Battery Capacity:** Honda has the highest average battery capacity among the three brands displayed, at 93 kWh.
3. **Driving Range:** Porsche leads the displayed brand comparison, with an average range of approximately 326 miles.
4. **Safety Ratings:** Volvo has the highest displayed average safety rating, at 4.75 out of 5.
5. **Charging Speed:** Porsche has the highest displayed average charging speed, at 279 kW.
6. **Yearly Trend:** The displayed EV count peaks at 70 in 2025 and is 62 in 2026.
