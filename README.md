# EV Cars Market Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring electric vehicle specifications and market data.

## Objectives
- Analyse EV brands, models and vehicle segments.
- Explore battery capacity, driving range and charging speed.
- Compare vehicle specifications and customer ratings.
- Identify useful patterns in EV market data.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV dataset

## Dashboard Features
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Brand and vehicle comparisons
- Battery, range and charging analysis
- Vehicle segment analysis

## Key Insights
EV Sales Performance: The dashboard reports total annual sales of 259M, providing an overview of electric vehicle sales performance across brands.

Battery Capacity: Honda has the highest average battery capacity among the three brands shown in the battery capacity chart, at 93 kWh.

Driving Range: Porsche leads the displayed brand comparison with an average driving range of approximately 326 miles.

Safety Ratings: Volvo has the highest average safety rating among the brands shown, at 4.75 out of 5.

Charging Speed: Porsche has the highest average charging speed among the brands displayed, at 279 kW.

EV Count by Year: The displayed vehicle count rises from 30 in 2020 to a peak of 70 in 2025, before declining to 62 in 2026.
